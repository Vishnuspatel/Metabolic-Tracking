As part of my freelance data work, the client desired a comprehensive report to track his overall health. Combatting a rare disease and doing a darn good job, the client has partially rejected standard treatment and opted to pursue other treatment options. Compared to folks his age, as well as others with the disease, the client is in relatively good shape and hopes to use his sharp mind to dive deeper into his physical health data to hopefully inform future treatment plans and help others going through the same battle. Stage one of the project has involved tracking metrics like ketones, glucose, and glucose-ketone index (GKI) over the past couple of years and automating the process so the client can track and analyze his own readings in real time. The process was automated in Python, and the report is then updated in Tableau. This project aims to expand further by incorporating other data sources, such as Apple Health and WHOOP, to explore how changes in lifestyle have affected his overall physical health.
